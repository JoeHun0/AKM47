AKM-47 by JoeHun0
=================

The good old 7.62x39 Kalashnikov for S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2. The game ships complete 7.62x39 ammo
(FMJ, AP, hollow-point) but no gun fires it and nothing ever spawns it. This mod adds the AKM-47:
its own AK-47 model on the AKM-74S rig, its own upgrade tree, and 7.62x39 across the Zone.

The gun
- Own 3D model with wooden furniture, plus its own inventory, upgrade-screen and PDA pictures.
  Reload, cocking, the moving bolt carrier and the rounds in the magazine all animate.
- Compared to the AKM-74S: damage 23 -> 30, armor piercing 1.0 -> 1.8, about 25% more recoil,
  a bit less accurate, shorter effective range, jams less often. 3.6 kg, price 12000.
- Takes the AKM-74S attachments: suppressor, flash suppressor, 4x scope, red dot, grenade launcher.
  (The 5.45 extended and coupled magazines don't fit - it's a 7.62x39 gun.)
- 7.62x39 is cheaper than in vanilla (FMJ 74 -> 30, AP/hollow-point 148 -> 46 per round), packs of 30.

Upgrade tree (18 upgrades on all five parts, plus the flash suppressor and offset aiming)
- Ideas from real 7.62x39 AKs: AKM stamped receiver, hammer rate reducer, chrome-lined bore,
  RPK heavy barrel, AKMS under-folding stock, laminated birch stock, extended safety lever...
- Either/or choices on several levels, so two AKM-47s can end up quite different.
- Every technician who can upgrade the AKM-74S can upgrade the AKM-47.

Where you find it
- NPCs: the AKM-47 joins the gun pool of generic stormtroopers of the eastern factions
  (Bandits, Loners, Duty, Military, Monolith, Noon, Spark, Ward, Corpus): Experienced 10%,
  Veteran 15%, Master 8%. Story/quest characters keep their own gear.
- Traders: wherever the AKM-74S is sold (Sidorovich, Chemical Plant, Asylum, Sultansk, Shevchenko,
  Rostok, Eger), and Guron (Slag Heap) always has one. Sold unloaded, full condition.
- 7.62x39 ammo: sold exactly like 5.45x39 (same traders, amounts and odds - FMJ, AP, hollow-point).
- Loot: FMJ in medium stashes, Soviet ammo crates and caches; AP in rare stashes, hollow-point in
  Veteran/Master rare stashes - next to 5.45x39, same odds, a bit fewer rounds.
- "Smart" loot (easier difficulties) gives 7.62x39 for the AKM-47 on bodies and in stashes. This also
  fixes a vanilla bug: its 7.62x39 slot handed out 7.62x54R sniper rounds.

Compatibility
- Everything is added on top of the game's lists (patches, nothing replaced), so it works next to
  other loot, trader and NPC gun mods. NPCs who don't roll the AKM-47 use their normal loadout.
- It overrides three AKM-74S animation collections (adds the AKM-47 magazine to them). Another mod
  that replaces the AKM-74S animation collections would conflict.
- Items, NPCs and trader stock that already spawned in a save keep what they had; new spawns and
  trader restocks bring the AKM-47. Can be added to an existing save.
- Before removing the mod, sell or drop any AKM-47 you carry - modded items can vanish or cause
  problems in a save without the mod.
- Made with Zone Kit Phase 2, tested on game version 2.0.x. As with all mods, achievements are disabled.

Languages: English, Ukrainian, and Simplified Chinese.
